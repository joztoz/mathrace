## Description (English)

Math Race Kids turns math practice into a fast, fun race!

Pick the correct answer as quickly as you can, build a streak and watch your times improve. Perfect for practicing the multiplication tables at home, in the car or before school.

FEATURES
• Quick multiplication practice with big, kid-friendly buttons
• Results and best times for every round
• Sounds, confetti and praise for correct answers
• English and Slovak
• Apple Watch app for practice on the wrist

MATH RACE PRO (optional one-time purchase, not a subscription)
• Duel mode – two players on one device: who hits STOP first?
• Division, addition, subtraction and mixed practice
• Tables up to 12
• Results and progress statistics

FOR PARENTS
• No ads, no tracking, no data collection
• Purchases and links outside the app are protected by a parental gate
• The free version stays fully playable
