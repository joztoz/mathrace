Privacy Policy

Math Race Kids · Last updated: November 8, 2026
In short: Math Race Kids does not collect, transmit, share or sell any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

Data we collect

None. The app (iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch) has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. It does not send any data over the internet.

Data stored on your device

Game settings (times table size, practice type, number of rounds, sounds, language) and recent results are saved only on your device. They are removed when you delete the app.

In-app purchase

The optional one-time purchase “Math Race PRO” is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, Apple Account or payment details. The app only asks the App Store whether PRO has been purchased.

Children

Math Race Kids is made for children and complies with the App Store Kids Category rules. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. Purchases and links that leave the app are protected by a parental gate.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
