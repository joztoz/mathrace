Math Race Kids Support

Fast math practice for kids on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch
Need help, found a bug or have an idea? Write to apps@maddog.sk. We usually reply within a few days.

How do I play?

  1. Tap Let's start! and pick the correct answer as fast as you can.
  2. In Duel mode two players share one device: whoever taps STOP first answers. A wrong answer lets the other player steal the point.
  3. On iPad or an unfolded iPhone Duo, Face to face splits the screen in half (on Duo at the fold). Two kids sit opposite each other, each solves their own problems, and the first to reach the goal wins.
  4. Open the gear icon to change the times table size, number of rounds, sounds and language.

What does PRO include?

Math Race PRO is a one-time in-app purchase (not a subscription). It unlocks Duel and Face to face modes, division, addition, subtraction and mixed practice, tables up to 12 and results statistics. The free version stays fully playable.

Why does the app ask me to type a number?

That is the parental gate. Before any purchase and before any link that leaves the app, an adult has to type a number that is written out in words. This keeps children from buying things or leaving the app by accident.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings (gear icon) → PRO → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account that bought PRO. Family Sharing is supported.

Does the app collect data or show ads?

No. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no accounts. See the Privacy Policy.
